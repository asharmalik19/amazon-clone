{# The header's cart: the icon that gets a shopper to `/cart`, and the badge saying how much is in it. The badge is its own macro because two places render it -- this link on a full page load, and the fragment an add-to-cart returns -- and a count that disagreed with itself between the two would be worse than no badge at all. `id="cart-count"` is the handle HTMX swaps it by, so it lives here next to the markup it names. `count` may be `None`, meaning "this page did not ask the database". The badge is then omitted rather than drawn as zero: a page that does not know what is in the cart should not claim it is empty. That is the error pages, which have no shell context -- see app/errors.py for why they deliberately ask the database nothing. #} {# The count sits *inside* the basket, which is the only clear space on the icon: level with the top of the wheels and centred on the basket rather than the whole glyph, because the handle hangs off the left and would otherwise pull the number onto the stroke.
