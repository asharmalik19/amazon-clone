{# A 4.6 rating is not four stars and it is not five, so the row is drawn twice: an outlined row underneath, and a filled row on top clipped to the exact fraction. That renders a partial star truthfully with no image assets and no rounding lie. Both rows are the same orange, the one sampled out of amazon_screenshots/ (see the `--color-amz-star` note in base.html). The empty half of a star is an outline rather than a grey silhouette, which is what the screenshots show and is also the more honest drawing: the same shape in the same colour, filled or not, reads as "this much of five" instead of as two different kinds of star. `rating` is display data (see app/models.py), so the fraction may be a float here -- unlike a price, a star row is never summed.
